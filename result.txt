## 1. Delivery Time Performance Analysis – Conclusion

The analysis of 45,584 food delivery orders revealed that the average delivery time was 26.29 minutes, while the median delivery time was 26 minutes. The most frequent delivery-time interval was 20–30 minutes.

Approximately 29.84% of orders took more than 30 minutes, indicating that a considerable proportion of deliveries experienced longer delivery times.

The traffic analysis revealed a strong association between traffic density and delivery performance:

- Jam traffic: Highest average delivery time at 31.18 minutes, with 50.63% of orders exceeding 30 minutes.
- High traffic: Average delivery time of 27.24 minutes, with 33.57% of orders exceeding 30 minutes.
- Medium traffic: Average delivery time of 26.70 minutes, with 32.47% of orders exceeding 30 minutes.
- Low traffic: Lowest average delivery time at 21.27 minutes, with only 7.87% of orders exceeding 30 minutes.

These findings suggest that traffic congestion is an important operational factor associated with longer delivery times.

Business Recommendation: Food delivery operations could investigate traffic-aware route planning, better rider allocation during peak congestion,
and strategies to reduce delays under heavy traffic conditions.

Overall, this analysis establishes a baseline for delivery performance and identifies traffic congestion as a key area for further operational investigation.



2. Weather Impact Analysis – Conclusion

The analysis revealed that weather conditions are associated with differences in food delivery performance.

Fog and Cloudy conditions recorded the highest average delivery times, at approximately 28.91 and 28.92 minutes, respectively. 
They also had the highest percentage of orders exceeding 30 minutes, at approximately 43.79%.

In contrast, Sunny conditions recorded the lowest average delivery time of 21.86 minutes, with only 12.52% of orders taking more than 30 minutes.

Further analysis combining weather and traffic conditions revealed that Fog under Jam traffic had an average delivery time of 36.81 minutes, compared with 23.08 minutes under Sunny and Jam conditions. 
This represents a difference of 13.73 minutes.

These findings suggest that weather conditions may be an important factor associated with delivery performance, particularly when combined with traffic congestion.

Business Recommendation: Delivery operations should investigate additional precautions and route-planning strategies during foggy and cloudy conditions, especially during heavy traffic.

Overall, weather conditions provide another important dimension for understanding delivery-time variation and identifying opportunities for operational improvement.
